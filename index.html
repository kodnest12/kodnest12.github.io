The Studio

A nest built from curiosity & code

KodNest Apps started the way most good things do — with a stubborn itch. A phone full of bloated finance apps that did too much and understood too little. So we built Expenzee: the tracker we actually wanted to open every day. Then Quran Kareem followed, because the most important app in a Muslim's day deserves better.

We are an independent studio based in Pakistan, publishing on Google Play. We keep our app footprint small so each experience can get obsessive attention — fast updates, honest privacy policies and design that respects your time.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Are KodNest apps free to use?

Yes. All features that matter are free, forever. Expenzee shows light, non-intrusive ads to keep development sustainable — and they never stand between you and your data.

Does Expenzee work without internet?

Absolutely. Expenzee is offline-first: you can log expenses in guest mode without any account. Sign in with Google when you want automatic, encrypted cloud backup across devices.

What happens to my financial data?

Your records stay yours. Nothing is sold to third parties, data is encrypted in transit, and you can delete your account and cloud data anytime from the app settings.

How do I get support or report a bug?

Write to apps.kodnest@gmail.com and you'll hear back from a human — usually within a day. Feedback actually drives the roadmap.

Will there be more apps?

Yes — more nests are in the works, all with the same DNA: simple, private, beautifully fast. Follow the studio on Google Play to be the first to know.
